Privacy Policy

personal-backup is used only by its owner to manage personal backups with rclone and Google Drive.

Data Access and Use

The application accesses the owner's Google Drive files with the owner's authorization. Access may include listing, reading, uploading, updating, or deleting files as directed by the owner.

Data Storage and Transfer

Files are transferred between the owner's systems and Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration and are not published.

Data Sharing

The application is not offered to other users. It does not sell personal data or use Google Drive data for advertising.

Revoking Access

The owner can revoke the application's Google Drive access through Google Account security settings.
